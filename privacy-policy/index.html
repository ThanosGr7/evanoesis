2. Important Privacy Principles

We aim to process only the data needed to operate, secure, and improve Code Evryma.

We use privacy by design and privacy by default principles. Findings, scan history, profile content, media, and precise location records are private to your account unless you choose to share them or enable a feature that clearly explains sharing.

Some data may be pseudonymised, encrypted, aggregated, or anonymised. Pseudonymised data can still be personal data if it can reasonably be linked back to a person or account. We treat account data, private findings, precise location data, media, notes, AI logs, purchase entitlement data, and security logs as personal data unless they have been properly anonymised.

We do not ask for your legal name, surname, postal address, government ID, or identity documents for normal App account creation.

3. Data We Collect

The exact data we collect depends on the features you use, the permissions you grant, your device settings, and the purchases you make.

3.1 Account Data

When you create or use an account, we may process:

  • username;
  • country;
  • password authentication credentials;
  • confirmation that you are 18 or older;
  • internal account ID;
  • account settings;
  • optional phone number for account recovery;
  • login, authentication, and session information.

We do not store your password in plain text. Passwords are stored using appropriate security measures such as hashing.

The optional phone number is used only for account recovery and related security purposes. If you do not provide a phone number, account recovery may be limited.

3.2 User Profile Content

You may choose to add content to your profile or account, including:

  • notes;
  • profile descriptions;
  • photos;
  • videos;
  • uploaded media;
  • other files or content you choose to provide.

Do not upload sensitive personal data, legal documents, identity documents, private information about other people, or content you do not have the right to upload.

Uploaded photos and videos may contain metadata, such as creation date, device information, or location metadata. Where practical and not needed for the feature, we remove or minimise unnecessary metadata.

3.3 Finding, Scan, and Location Data

When you use detection, map, finding, or location features, we may process:

  • GPS coordinates or approximate location;
  • map pins;
  • scan records;
  • finding categories, such as “iron” or other material indicators;
  • detection notes;
  • timestamps;
  • detection statistics;
  • related photos, videos, or notes;
  • user choices about whether findings are private or shared.

Private findings and private location records are stored for your account functionality and are not shared publicly by default.

3.4 Optional Shared Finding Data

You may have the option to share finding-related data with Evanoesis to improve the App and service quality.

Shared finding data may include:

  • location or approximate location;
  • finding type or material category;
  • scan quality information;
  • device or sensor context;
  • related non-identifying technical metadata;
  • optional notes or media if you choose to share them.

This sharing is optional and controlled by an in-app setting. Where exact coordinates are not necessary, we use approximate location, generalised coordinates, geohashes, aggregation, or other minimisation methods. Before shared finding data is used for analytics or service improvement, we separate it from account identifiers where practical.

3.5 AI Feature Data

If you use AI features, we may process:

  • prompts and questions you submit;
  • images, text, notes, or other inputs you provide to the AI feature;
  • AI responses and outputs;
  • AI usage events;
  • Credit consumption linked to AI usage;
  • AI memory settings, if enabled;
  • error and performance logs.

AI inputs and outputs may be processed by our servers and, where applicable, by AI service providers acting under contract. Do not submit sensitive personal data, financial data, legal secrets, private information about other people, or unlawful content into AI features.

3.6 Purchase and Credit Data

All payments are handled by Apple App Store or Google Play. We do not directly collect or store your card number, bank account, or payment credentials.

To deliver purchases, Credits, unlocks, and memberships, we may process:

  • transaction ID or receipt identifier;

  • refund, cancellation, or chargeback status where provided by the platform;
  • Credit balance;
  • Credit usage history;

Apple and Google may process payment and app store account data under their own terms and privacy policies.

Where possible, we use safeguards such as:

11. Account Deletion and Erase-All-Content Requests

The App includes account and content controls in Settings.

11.1 Delete Account Forever

Use this option to request permanent deletion of your Code Evryma account and associated personal data from active systems.

In-app path:

Settings → Account → Delete Account Forever

Android users and users who cannot access the App may also use the public account deletion web link:

https://www.evanoesis.com/account-deletion

The web deletion page explains how to request deletion and what data will be deleted or retained.

11.2 Erase All Content

Use this option to erase user-generated content while keeping your account active.

In-app path:

Settings → Account → Erase All Content

This may delete profile notes, uploaded media, private findings, private map pins, scan history, and similar content. It does not delete the account itself, Credits balance, membership entitlement, purchase-verification records, security logs, or legally required records.

11.3 Limits on Deletion

We may retain limited records where required or permitted by law, including for:

  • tax and accounting;
  • purchase verification;
  • refunds, chargebacks, or disputes;
  • fraud prevention;
  • security investigations;
  • compliance with legal obligations;
  • enforcement of Terms;
  • protection of rights, safety, and legal claims.

If you have an active membership or subscription, deleting your Code Evryma account may not automatically cancel the app store subscription. You must cancel the subscription through Apple App Store or Google Play where required.
